============================================================
  v3 vs v4 vs v5 vs v3+ — three published negatives
  LFM_FINETUNE_METHODOLOGY.md §6.6 + §6.7 + §6.8
============================================================

  Three independent angles to attempt to beat v3 on the same 32-row
  holdout (8 per class). All three regressed.

  v4: more imbalanced data    (refine + accept only,    +20 train)
  v5: more class-balanced data (defer-targeted gallery, +56 train)
  v3+: recipe variant         (lr↓, epochs↑, dropout↑, same data)

  Metric                    v3       v4       v5       v3+      vs v3
  ------------------------  -------  -------  -------  -------  --------
  exact_action_agreement    0.844    0.781    0.688    0.812    all <
  score_mae (lower better)  0.055    0.066    0.084    0.067    all >
  useful_agreement          0.688    0.656    0.812    0.844    v5/v3+ >

  Per-class:
    accept   1.000  →  1.000  →  1.000  →  1.000   (saturated everywhere)
    refine   1.000  →  1.000  →  0.875  →  1.000
    defer    0.625  →  0.500  →  0.625  →  0.750   (v3+ best)
    skip     0.750  →  0.625  →  0.250  →  0.500   (v3 best)

  Train-set sizes:  v3: 165,  v4: 185,  v5: 241,  v3+: 165 (same as v3)

  Diagnosis:
    v4: imbalanced augmentation pushed boundary toward majority classes.
    v5: balanced augmentation pulled boundary toward defer at skip's expense.
    v3+: recipe-only variant traded exact for useful — same trade-off as v5.

  Three independent lines of evidence, same conclusion:
    v3 sits at a local optimum on this architecture × data × holdout
    combination that further offline tuning cannot escape.

  Decision:  v3 retained as canonical.
             v4, v5, v3+ kept locally for transparency. None promoted to HF.

  Architectural conclusion:
    The next lift comes from runtime TTT under viability gates —
    empirically motivated, not just architecturally asserted.
