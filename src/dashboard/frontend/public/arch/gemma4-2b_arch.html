GEMMA 4 2B · WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING AT

Three modes, all based on the same ground-truth tensor manifest extracted from the GGUF file. Every shape is falsifiable: open tensor_manifest.json to verify.

Axes (all modes)

X axis (left → right)Layer index 0–34. x=−4 holds global (non-layer) tensors.
Y axis (front → back)Tensor type, grouped by subsystem: Attention → FFN → AltUp.
Z axis (height)Proportional to log₂(parameters), scaled by 0.76 and offset by −17. Read actual param counts from the tick labels, not the raw Z value.

Tensor subsystems

attn_q / k / v / outSelf-attention projections. Q and O double in width at global-attention layers.
ffn_gate / up / downFeed-forward (SwiGLU). Hidden dim doubles from 6144 → 12288 at layer 15 and stays there.
altup_scaleAltUp.correct_output_scale — scalar [1] per layer. AltUp correction magnitude. Tiny bar height = min(log₂ scale).
inp_gate / projper_layer_input_gate [1536→256] + per_layer_projection [256→1536]. Last block operation. Constant 256-dim.

Architectural phases

Layers 0–14Small FFN (6144). Global attn at L4, L9, L14.
Layers 15–34Large FFN (12288). Global attn continues at L19, L24, L29, L34. Layers 19/24/29/34 have both.

Floor plates

INFERENCE mode

Simulates a single token's forward pass. Each layer resolves into 6 sub-phases matching Gemma3nTextDecoderLayer.forward() exactly (verified against transformers source):

Phase 1 — Q · K · VAttention projections. AltUp.predict() and the Laurel block also fire here — their weights are not in the GGUF (llama.cpp doesn't export them). Global layers pulse white.
Phase 2 — attn_outQKT/√d → softmax → weighted V → output projection back to d_model.
Phase 3 — FFN gate + upSwiGLU expand: gate_proj × up_proj → hidden dim (6144 or 12288).
Phase 4 — FFN downCompress back to d_model. Laurel block output also merges into residual here (per source), but Laurel weights are not in the GGUF — this merge is invisible.
Phase 5 — altup_scaleAltUp.correct_output_scale — a single scalar [1] per layer in the GGUF. Tiny bar, real tensor.
Phase 6 — inp_gate + projper_layer_input_gate [1536→256] + per_layer_projection [256→1536]. Last operation in the block.

Glow flows up the Y-axis (attn → FFN → AltUp → per-layer gate) before advancing to the next layer. 210 sub-steps (35 × 6). Drag slider to scrub. Source: modeling_gemma3n.py lines 1410–1477.

What is NOT in the GGUF (llama.cpp omits)

Laurel blocklinear_left [d_model→laurel_rank], linear_right [laurel_rank→d_model]. Runs alongside attention; merged into residual after FFN.
AltUp modality_routerRoutes across altup_num_inputs stacked embeddings. Predict + correct coefficients not exported.

These are novel Gemma 3n architecture features. The GGUF represents the llama.cpp-runnable subset of the model.
